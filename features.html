All features

Every Feature, Group by Group.

Open a group to read it. One line a feature, only what works today.

OverviewThe Overview Page10
  • Net worth. What you own less what you owe, in EGP.
  • Free cash. What is yours to use after the bills you owe.
  • What it is made of. Cash, gold, shares, funds and certificates side by side.
  • If you sold today. An estimate of what each kind of asset would bring in cash, with a sale factor you can set.
  • Needs you. One list of every statement to review and every entry without a category, beside bills due, missing prices and a plan short of what you mean to save.
  • Cash flow and savings rate. What came in, what went out and what you kept.
  • Where it went. Your spending by category for any period.
  • Month by month. How each month compares with the others.
  • Explain a figure. Click a figure on the Overview to see how it adds up.
  • Financial health. Savings rate, emergency fund in months and debt to net worth against limits you set, with six months over time.
Phone and PCYour Phone and PC7
  • Your phone holds the ledger. Tap + to add spending wherever you are.
  • The same figures. Overview, accounts and cash planning, in a layout made for the phone.
  • Pair once. Type the phone’s code on your PC; both screens then show the same six check digits.
  • Your PC borrows it. Encrypted over your own Wi-Fi, opened with the same password as on your phone.
  • One edits, the other reads. While it is lent, the phone shows Lent to Office PC · read only.
  • Hand it back. Close Lightning near your phone and the ledger goes home; away from it, the PC’s edits are sealed for the next meeting.
  • Bank SMS into review. After you allow it, new messages from your banks wait for you; nothing posts until you say so.
Accounts and transactionsAccounts and Transactions11
  • Accounts. Bank, cash, wallet and investment accounts, each with a starting balance.
  • Money in, money out and transfers. A transfer is never counted as spending.
  • Split a purchase. Across several categories.
  • Edit, void and restore. A wrong click never loses a record.
  • Select many. Then categorise or delete them in one go.
  • Search with Ctrl K. One box finds a page, an account, a name, a category, an investment or a #tag.
  • #tags. A #tag in a note gathers its transactions, with their money in and money out.
  • Check against the bank. A small difference becomes one adjustment; a bigger one is reviewed row by row.
  • Export selected rows. Take the entries you choose out of a register.
  • Counterparties. One name for each place you pay or get paid, with a default category.
  • Rules. Set the category, tags or split by name, note, amount or account, in Settings.
Money held for othersMoney Held for Others3
  • Held for others. Money and units you hold for someone else, shown apart from what you own.
  • Cash ownership. Reassign cash between you and a saved person without changing the account's total.
  • Paid on your behalf. Record an expense someone else paid and still count it in your spending.
CSV importBank Statement CSV Import8
  • Bank CSV import. One signed amount column, or separate in and out columns.
  • Column mapping. Match the columns once, then review.
  • Review before posting. Only rows that need a decision need your attention.
  • One decision per new name. A name typed on several rows is created once.
  • Duplicates flagged. So the same row is not imported twice.
  • Link to an entry you already made. So the same money is never counted twice.
  • Name suggestions. Similar names are offered for you to pick, never merged silently.
  • Import helper. A blank template and a ready prompt to shape any statement with the tool you choose.
BudgetBudget by Category9
  • Amount or percent. Type 1,500, or 12% of your average monthly income.
  • Average of recent months. Use what you usually spend as the plan.
  • Carryover. See what the previous month left, category by category.
  • Any period. All time, year to date, a month or a custom range.
  • Loan payments. Planned for you until you set your own amount.
  • Bulk plans. Apply a plan to many categories at once, and undo it.
  • Fill this month. Plans the month in one click, up to the most you can plan.
  • Your savings target. The plan leaves room for it, your goals and the emergency fund, and says how much to cut if not.
  • Categories in three levels. Broad groups, categories and optional detail.
Cash planningSafe to Spend, Bills and Forecast13
  • Safe to spend. Your cash until the next income, after what you owe.
  • What you owe. Bills due and loans still to pay.
  • Next 30 days and three-month forecast. Every payment and income ahead.
  • Recurring bills, subscriptions and income. Suggested from your history, never created on their own.
  • A payment finds its bill. A clear match settles it; an early or changed amount waits for you.
  • Weekend bills. A bill due on Friday or Saturday moves to Thursday or Sunday.
  • Skip a payment. And undo the skip.
  • Loans. Progress and payoff date.
  • Debt and fixed-cost ratios. Debt to net worth, debt to cash, loan payments to income and fixed costs to income.
  • Reserves. Set cash aside for a goal and track what you have set aside.
  • Goals with a date. Each says what it needs a month, and the budget and forecast count it.
  • Emergency fund. Counted in months of income or of spending; its monthly top-up comes off safe to spend.
  • Certificate interest and maturity. Shown in the forecast, apart from income.
InvestmentsShares and Funds12
  • Buy, sell and dividends. Recorded inside your brokerage account.
  • Prices. Typed in, fetched, or filled from the price files of the markets you follow.
  • Egyptian Exchange catalogue. Pick shares and funds by name.
  • US stocks and ETFs. About 400 stocks and 70 ETFs on Nasdaq, NYSE and Arca, picked by name and priced in dollars.
  • Gulf and European shares. Pick the exchange and type the price; the currency follows the exchange.
  • Foreign gain in two parts. A dollar holding shows its price gain and its exchange-rate gain apart.
  • Net gain or loss. Gain from sales, price change on what you hold and dividends, in one figure.
  • Yearly return (XIRR). Based on when your money went in, shown once a holding is a year old.
  • Holdings after sale. An estimate of what you would keep after selling.
  • Every price change recorded. Monthly revaluations and sale-day adjustments, each one traceable.
  • Target allocation. Set the share you want in each kind of asset.
  • Investment plan helper. Shows how to split new money towards your targets.
CurrenciesDollars and Other Currencies4
  • Hold a currency. Dollar and other balances stay in their own currency beside your EGP.
  • Convert between currencies. Records the rate you actually got.
  • Rates with their age. A rate you typed or traded beats a market rate, and an old rate says how old it is.
  • Exchange-rate gain. For each currency: average rate, gain not yet realised, gain realised and the spread you paid.
Gold and certificatesGold and Bank Certificates7
  • Gold by piece. Net weight, karat and cost for each piece, valued per gram of the same karat.
  • Buy and sell gold. Each purchase and sale is recorded against the piece.
  • Certificates. Principal, rate, simple or compound, payout, earliest withdrawal and maturity.
  • Maturity by date or term. Type a date, or a term in years.
  • Interest forecast. An estimate only; you enter the real interest from your bank statement.
  • Redeem a certificate. Enter what the bank actually paid back.
  • Bank portfolios. Certificates grouped under the bank that issued them.
Privacy and backupsPrivacy, Backups and Recovery9
  • Windows app. No Python or other software needed.
  • Profiles. Named, each encrypted and opened with its own password.
  • Recovery. A 12-digit recovery key and a security question reset a forgotten password.
  • Backups. One is made every time the app starts, and you can back up whenever you like.
  • Restore (early preview). Choose an encrypted backup from the lock screen and bring it back.
  • Data checks. Compare totals across your ledger and reports.
  • Export your numbers. One workbook prepared on your device; nothing is uploaded.
  • Privacy mode. The eye beside Search blurs every amount on screen.
  • Start fresh. A new empty profile, with the old one left exactly as it was.
ShortcutsTyping Shortcuts and Everyday Settings9
  • Sums in amount fields. Type 120+35*2 and it saves 190.
  • Dates. Type 31/1 or 2026-01-31, or pick from a calendar.
  • English and Egyptian Arabic. Switch the language in Settings; Arabic screens read right to left, with amounts and dates in their natural order.
  • Arabic digits. Amounts and dates typed with Arabic-Indic digits are understood.
  • Type and pick. Choose your own accounts, categories and names by typing.
  • Month picker. Pick a month; never type one.
  • Answers first. Up to three one-line answers under each page title.
  • Light and dark. Follows your device.
  • Sample household. Try everything on a sample before you enter your own.
