01 / mission

Why we opened the code.

Lightning shows a salaried person in Egypt where their money stands, what is coming, and why. Bank, wallet, cash at home, certificates, THNDR and gold, in EGP, on their own PC and phone.

A money app asks for trust. We would rather show our working. With the source public, anyone can check how a figure is calculated, how the data is locked, and what happens when something breaks.

And the more people can read it, the faster it gets better for every Egyptian who plans with it.

Who we are. Lightning started with one person who logged every pound by hand for years, and wanted a tool that showed it all in one place (the story). Today the founder, a small team and three AI teammates build it in public, through the same pull requests you would open.

  • [free]Free to download and useno account, no subscription
  • [local]Your ledger stays on your devicesphone and PC pair directly, no cloud
  • [public]Read it, run it, change itPolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0: source-available, free for non-commercial use
[secret] the other reason

Everything on the left is true. This is also true.

I work in finance. I can price a three-year certificate in my head and tell you what a gram of 21-karat did last March. What I can't tell you is why a test that passed at 11 p.m. fails at 2 a.m. with no change in between.

After enough of those nights, opening the code stopped being a principle and became a plan: if more people can read it, fewer of the bugs are mine to find alone.

So yes, transparency. And also: please send help. Pull requests are cheaper than therapy.

04 / stack

One process. One file.

A single-user modular monolith: one Python process, one SQLite database, pages rendered on the server. A backup is a copy.

  • ui/FastAPI routes, Jinja, vanilla JS. No SQL, no maths.
  • workflows/Use-cases that span modules, in one transaction.
  • planning/Bills, loans, the cash forecast. Plans move no money.
  • domain services/Accounts, assets, transactions, investments, budgets.
  • reporting/Read-only views, computed from the ledger.
  • database/SQLite, migrations, backup, audit log.
  • core/Money, dates, posting rules. Depends on nothing.

05 / gaps

What isn't done yet.

Good engineering is honest about its edges. These are ours, today. What comes after them is on the roadmap →

  • [beta]

    Still in testing. Ordinary-PC acceptance, legacy import and in-app backup restore are not finished. Test with sample data, and keep your own backup of anything real.

  • [unproven]

    Restore safety is tested, not proven: the reboot drill on an ordinary Windows PC is still to do, and power-loss behaviour is unverified.

  • [known]

    Freed memory is not wiped. Decrypted fragments may persist in freed memory, crash dumps or the page file. Nothing protects an unlocked PC from malware.

  • [partial]

    Statement import reads CSV today. PDF and Excel are next, and open to help.

  • [open]

    Routes are async def with synchronous database work inside. It works; it isn't right yet. Open to help too.

06 / help

Where we need you.

You don't need to know finance, or all of the code. Pick the one you know. Each links to real work, open today.

  • [arabic]

    Arabic and right to left. Lines that stay in English, figures that read out of order. If you read Arabic and can edit a template, start here: #37, #34.

  • [screens]

    Screens and charts. Small fixes in Jinja, CSS and plain JavaScript, each with the file named: #33, #35, #36.

  • [python]

    Backend. PDF and Excel statements through the same review as CSV, and routes that are honestly sync or async. Python, FastAPI, Decimal, tests first.

  • [android]

    Android. The app runs on the phone through Chaquopy. Next is a Play-ready build: an App Bundle, Play App Signing and the API level Play asks for.

  • [testing]

    Your own PC and bank. Run the Windows build with sample data and file what breaks. Know a bank's SMS or CSV format we don't read? Open an issue with redacted samples, never real ones.
