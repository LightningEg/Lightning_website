Money held for others

Their Money. Not Your Net Worth.

Cash you hold for family stays in your account but out of what you own, so your net worth is the truth.

Download the free beta See all features

Free to use. Windows. No bank login.

The problem

Your Balance Isn’t All Yours.

You collect rent for a sibling, hold savings for your parents, or pay a bill someone will repay.

Held Apart From What You Own

Mark money in your account as held for someone you have saved. It stays in the account total but leaves your net worth.

Reassign Without Moving Cash

Change who owns part of an account between you and a saved person, without changing the account’s total.

Paid on Your Behalf, Still Counted

When someone pays an expense for you, record it. It still counts in your spending.

Mohab’s example

A Real Number from Our Sample Household.

Mohab holds 6,000 EGP for family inside his accounts.

In the Account

The 6,000 EGP sits in his bank balance, so the bank statement still matches.

Out of His Net Worth

It is subtracted from what Mohab owns, so his net worth never counts family money as his.

Good questions

Questions, Answered

Does money I hold for others count in my net worth?

No. It stays in the account but is shown apart and left out of what you own.

Does the account balance still match my bank?

Yes. Reassigning ownership between you and a saved person doesn’t change the account’s total.

What about an expense someone else paid for me?

Record it as paid on your behalf. It still counts in your spending.

Can I track money I lent out?

Not in the current beta.
