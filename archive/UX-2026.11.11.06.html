Lightning or A traditional expense tracker?

A phone app where you log spending by hand or from bank SMS, usually free with ads or a small one-off fee.

Lightning or An AI assistant?

A chat assistant you ask about your money in plain words, usually on a free tier or a monthly subscription, with what you type sent to the company's servers.
