M1 / now

Finish the core. Open the workflow.

Two tracks at the same time: make the backend complete and the features whole, and set up how people outside the team can build with us. The detailed plan behind it has nine numbered milestones: a task titled [3C] is item C of milestone 3, and the codes on the items below point into it.

8 of 21 done · 6 in progress, counted from the items below · the whole plan: issues/INDEX.md ↗

1a · backend and featuresthe app works, end to end

  • [done]

    The core of v1.1Ledger, budget, reports, investments, certificates and gold, cash planning, bank CSV import, Arabic. One ledger, every figure traceable.

  • [done]

    Phone and PC, pairedEncrypted profiles on both, paired directly with no cloud. Bank SMS for CIB and NBE, review-only.

  • [now]

    Ordinary-PC acceptance · 2CRecovery and fault drills on an ordinary Windows PC, including the reboot drill and power-loss evidence.

  • [now]

    Legacy import and in-app restore · 2A, 2C2Bring old data in, and restore a backup from inside the app, both proven end to end.

  • [next]

    PDF and Excel statements · 7BRead them through the same CSV review: size caps, no macros, no formulas run.

  • [next]

    A trusted price source for Europe and the Gulf · 6BLicensed or live-verified prices for European and GCC shares. The adapters exist; the source does not yet.

  • [next]

    Currencies that match across banks · 7EPair bank-imported transfers when the two sides are in different currencies.

  • [next]

    Search that knows where you are · 7DScoped pickers, alias history and ranking on top of today's Ctrl-K.

  • [next]

    Honest asyncRoutes are async def with synchronous database work inside. Make them one or the other.

  • [later]

    Windows in-app updates · 9BAn Update button: verify the signature, swap folders, keep the old one for rollback, never touch profiles.

  • [later]

    The phone finds the PC by itselfDiscovery instead of typing an address, and periodic recovery copies between devices.

1b · open-source workflowanyone can help, safely

  • [done]

    Public source, plain termsEvery line on GitHub. Contribution terms in CONTRIBUTING.md: you keep your copyright.

  • [done]

    Rules a newcomer can followAGENTS.md, one plan in issues/ with a code for every item, and an architecture that tests enforce, not reviewers.

  • [now]

    A public roadmapThis page. Kept in step with NOW.md and the plan in issues/.

  • [now]

    Issue templatesBug or improvement, and Good first issue, are live. Bank SMS format next, with redacted samples only.

  • [now]

    Good first issuesSmall and self-contained, with the file to open named. Comment /take and a bot assigns it to you.

  • [done]

    CI on every pull requestTests, dependency review and CodeQL run on each PR; the template asks what changed and how you checked it.

  • [done]

    A security policySECURITY.md: report a flaw privately through GitHub, never in a public issue.

  • [done]

    Setup in one clickOpen in Codespaces: everything installs and the demo household opens in your browser.

  • [done]

    Clear review rulesCONTRIBUTING.md: who reviews, who merges, and what every change must pass.

  • [now]

    A home for contributorsEvery task, claim and question lives on its GitHub issue. A short monthly note on what landed next.

M2 / next

Publish on Google Play.

The Android app already runs from the same code. This milestone makes it a store app.

1 of 7 done · 1 in progress

play storeandroid first, it's what most of Egypt carries

  • [done]

    An Android app from the same codePython runs on the phone through Chaquopy. A signed arm64 APK is out to testers.

  • [now]

    The SMS permission declarationGoogle Play asks why the app reads SMS. Ours: bank messages, suggested as transactions, always confirmed by you.

  • [next]

    A Play-ready buildAn Android App Bundle with Play App Signing, 16 KB page support, and the target API level Play requires.

  • [next]

    Data safety, told plainlyNo account, no cloud, data stays on the device. The form and the privacy policy say the same thing.

  • [next]

    A store listing in two languagesEnglish and Egyptian Arabic, with real screens from Mohab's sample year.

  • [next]

    A closed testTesters from the WhatsApp and Discord groups on Play's closed track before anything goes public.

  • [later]

    Public releaseThen updates arrive through Google Play, like any other app.

M3 / later

Publish on the App Store.

The hardest port: the Python core, encrypted, on iPhone. Nothing here has started, and we say so.

0 of 6 done · 0 in progress

app storeiOS, after Play is out

  • [later]

    Python on iOSCPython supports iOS since 3.13. Build SQLCipher and cryptography for it, and never swap in plaintext SQLite to make it work.

  • [later]

    A native shell around the same screensThe same pages and the same rules, paired with the PC the same way.

  • [later]

    Face ID, optionalA third lock on the same key, like fingerprint on Android. Password and recovery key always still work.

  • [later]

    TestFlightAn Apple developer account and a TestFlight beta for testers.

  • [later]

    App Store reviewPrivacy labels and Apple's rules for finance apps, met before we submit.

  • [later]

    App Store releaseLightning on iPhone.
