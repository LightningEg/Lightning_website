Verify administrator access

Wallet credits, recognition points and post moderation need MFA. Set up an authenticator once, then verify when your admin session needs it.

Credit coins or recognition points

Coins fund purchases. Recognition points affect ranking but cannot be spent. This is a manual credit, not confirmation of an InstaPay payment. Give a clear reason.

Palette prices

Mono stays free. New locked starter palettes begin at 100 coins; change prices here without editing SQL.

Receipt retention

Remove images older than 90 days through Storage. Evidence for pending payments is protected.

Review uploaded receipts

Compare the member email in the transfer note, payer InstaPay address, reference and receipt with the actual incoming transfer in your own account. A picture alone does not verify payment.

Community stories

Review submitted stories and award recognition once per accepted story. LinkedIn links never award points automatically.
