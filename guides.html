Budget Your Salary

Pay the bills first, plan each category, keep a cushion, and spend what is safe.

  • How Do I Split a 20,000 EGP Salary?One worked example, from rent to savings.
  • How Do I Build a Monthly Budget in EGP?A plan for each category that fits your salary, and what’s left every day.
  • Why Does My Salary Run Out Before the Month Ends?The spending you cannot see, and how to find it.
  • How Do I Budget When My Income Is Irregular?Build on the lowest income you can rely on.
  • How Much Is Safe to Spend Before Payday?Your cash, less what is already promised before the next salary.
  • How Much Do I Need in an Emergency Fund?Three to six months of essentials, and how to start small.

  • Where Does My Money Go? A Free Expense TrackerEvery pound out, by category, from your statements and the cash you spent.
  • Can I Budget From My Bank Statement Without Sharing My Login?Bring in a CSV, match its columns once and check every row.

Know What You Own

Add up every place your money sits, take off what you owe, and that is your net worth.

  • How Do I Work Out My Net Worth?Add up what you own in EGP, then take off what you owe and what isn’t yours.
  • How Do I Track Gold, Certificates and Shares Together?One portfolio in EGP: what it earned, and what you added.
  • How Do I Track Dollar Savings and US Shares in EGP?Dollars stay dollars, and add up in pounds at the day’s rate.
  • How Do I Track Money I Hold for Someone Else?Their money stays in your account, but out of your net worth.
