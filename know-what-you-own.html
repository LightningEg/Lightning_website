The pieces

Four Things That Make the Number Honest.

The short answer to each is here. The full guide is one tap away.

Count Everything Once.

Bank accounts, Vodafone Cash, cash at home, certificates, shares and gold, each in EGP at its latest value. Take off what you owe, and nothing is counted twice.

Work out your net worth

See What Your Investments Earned.

Gold by the gram, certificates at their balance and shares at their latest price, in one portfolio. Mohab’s is 752,486 EGP, and what it earned stays apart from the money he added.

Track gold, certificates and shares

Keep Dollars as Dollars.

A dollar account keeps its balance in dollars and adds up in EGP at the day’s rate. A holding’s gain splits into the price part and the rate part. Mohab’s dollar brokerage counts as 114,211 EGP.

Track dollar savings

Leave Out What Is Not Yours.

Money you hold for family stays in your account but out of your net worth. Mohab holds 6,900 EGP for family, and his balance still matches the bank.

Track money held for others
