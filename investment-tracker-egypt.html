Good questions

Investments, Answered

What it earned, and what you added.

What is net gain?

What your investments earned or lost in the period: gains from sales, the price change on what you still hold, and dividends and interest.

Is money I added the same as a gain?

No. Money you move in from your salary is saved, not earned. Lightning shows them apart: Mohab added 201,450 EGP this year and his investments gained 44,559 EGP.

What is the investing rate?

The share of what you kept that you moved into investments. Mohab kept 262,111 EGP this year, a 45.4% savings rate, and moved 201,450 EGP of it into investments: 76.9%. Money from earlier savings doesn’t count, so it never passes 100%.

Does it connect to THNDR or my bank?

No. Add your holdings and record buys and sales, or import a CSV. Lightning never asks for a broker or bank login.

Do my investments count in my net worth?

Yes, at their latest value, beside your cash and less what you owe. How net worth is worked out.

Does Lightning tell me what to buy?

No. It shows what you own and how it did. It doesn’t recommend investments.
