Install

Three Steps, and You’re In.

No account, and no bank password.

1

On your PC

Unzip the file into a new folder and open Lightning.exe inside it. Nothing to install, but Windows needs Microsoft Edge WebView2.

2

On your phone

A direct APK, no unzipping, from outside Google Play, so Android may warn you or ask to allow installs from your browser. Download it only from this page.

3

Pair the two

Open both apps on the same Wi-Fi. The phone keeps the ledger and the PC borrows it, encrypted and with no server.

This download is an APK installed outside Google Play. Android may show a verification warning or ask you to allow installation from your browser. Continue only if you trust Lightning and downloaded this file from our official release page. Never install an APK from a source you don’t know.

App appearance

Try Dendera.

Open Settings → Appearance and choose Dendera or Dendera, night. It applies immediately. Choose Lightning default to switch back.

Free and unlocked themes save with the profile. To keep a paid theme: buy it on the Marketplace, press Get app code on its card, then paste the code in Settings → Appearance → Unlock a theme you bought. Do this once per profile: the profile’s other devices unlock by themselves. Another profile needs its own unlock, at a quarter of the price. The store must enable the app release before it can issue a code.

One Dendera website purchase includes its day and night looks across this website and For Geeks. See the live price and the steps in Use it in the app.

Why Lightning exists

What My Own Numbers Taught Me

When I started investing, I chased percentages. The returns looked exciting, until I looked at the actual amounts, and at the worry and risk that came with them.

I had logged every pound since I graduated and reviewed it each week. That log showed me something quieter: fewer Uber rides and Talabat orders, and a little more care when shopping, kept almost as much each month as my investments made. And it kept doing it, month after month.

None of this is new. Most good money books land in the same place: you can’t control much around you, but you can control your own habits, and habits compound.

Keeping that log by hand took hours every month, and it still never showed everything in one place. Lightning is the tool I wanted then: everything I own and owe, counted properly, so the habits are easy to see. It’s still early, and it gets better with every tester.
