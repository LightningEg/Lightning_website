How Lightning counts it

Dollars Stay Dollars.

Until you spend or convert them.

Each Account, Its Own Currency

A dollar account keeps its balance in dollars. Lightning adds it to your net worth in EGP at the day’s rate, and keeps the rate it used for each day.

The Day’s Rate, or Yours

Rates come from Banque Misr’s daily list or online, or you type the rate your bank gave you, and yours wins. A rate older than ten days shows its date.

Convert Keeps What You Got

Change dollars to pounds, or back, and Lightning records the rate you actually got, the fee and the spread you paid.

Good questions

Dollars, Answered

What it counts, and what it doesn’t.

Which currencies can I keep?

Any. Each account has one currency, so dollars, euros, Saudi riyals or dirhams sit beside your pound accounts, and everything adds up in EGP.

Where do the exchange rates come from?

From Banque Misr’s daily rates or an online lookup, or the rate you type. A rate you type, or one you actually traded at, wins for its day. A rate older than ten days shows its date, and after 45 days Lightning asks you for a new one.

Is the dollar’s rise income?

No. It shows apart, as the gain from the rate. On dollars you still hold it isn’t locked in until you spend or convert them: Mohab’s 1,235 dollars are 2,023 EGP up on what they cost.

Which US shares get their prices by themselves?

About 400 of the largest US stocks and 70 ETFs on Nasdaq, NYSE and NYSE Arca, priced in dollars. For any other share, Gulf and European ones included, you type the price.
