How it works

Thirty Minutes. Once a Month.

At the end of the month, bring in your statements, tidy a few rows, then sit down with your numbers. Between sit-downs, Lightning’s Android beta catches spending as you go and keeps bank SMS waiting for your review.

Free to use. Windows and Android. No bank login.

Once, before your first month

Set Up in an Afternoon

  1. Add your accountsBanks, wallets, CDs, THNDR and gold, with today’s balances.
  2. Set a budgetA fixed amount, a share of income, or your usual average.
  3. Add what’s comingBills, subscriptions, loans and money you set aside.
1About 10 minutes

Upload Your Statements

Download last month’s CSV from each bank and wallet, and bring each one into its account. Lightning reads the usual columns itself; after that it’s a few clicks.

What you do

  1. Download the CSV from each bank or wallet.
  2. Choose Import CSV on the matching account.
  3. Standard columns are matched for you; anything else, once.

Good to knowOnly have a PDF? Lightning gives you ready-made instructions to turn it into a CSV with an AI assistant.

Lightning · Import statement

One file per account. Nothing is saved yet.

2About 5 minutes

Make Small Adjustments

Lightning only asks about what needs you: a new name, a possible duplicate, a row it can’t place. Settle those, post the rest, and add any cash you spent.

What you do

  1. Answer the rows that need a decision. One answer fills every row with that name.
  2. Post the ready rows.
  3. Add any cash spending the statement can’t see.

Good to knowTick “Use this category next time” and next month’s statement sorts itself.
