How they stay in step

Pair Once. Then It Follows You.

No account to make and nothing to upload. Your phone and your PC trust each other, and only each other.

  1. 1

    Pair Once

    Once

    Your phone shows a code; type it on your PC. Both screens then show the same six digits, such as 370 548, so you know it’s your phone.

  2. 2

    Borrow It

    Any time

    Open it on your PC with the same password as on your phone. It arrives with every edit you made on the go, and your phone reads while you work.

  3. 3

    Hand It Back

    On close

    Close Lightning near your phone and the ledger goes home. Away from it, your edits are sealed and wait for the next meeting.

Less typing

It Does the Tedious Part.

Say a thing once, and Lightning remembers it.

Bank SMS, Read for You

On your phone, new messages from your banks wait in review with the amount filled in. CIB and NBE in Arabic today. Messages from people are never read.

Rules That File for You

Say once where a name, an amount or a note belongs. Every import and every new row follows, split and tagged if you like.

Bills That Know the Weekend

A bill due on a Friday moves to Thursday. When its payment arrives, the bill finds it and settles by itself.

Goals Your Budget Knows

Each dated goal says what it needs a month. When the plan can’t reach it, you see how much to cut, or the month it can.

Financial Health

Savings rate, emergency fund in months and debt to net worth, each against a limit you set, with six months to compare.

Find Anything, Hide Everything

Ctrl-K finds an account, a name or a #tag. Amounts take sums like 120+35*2. One click blurs every amount, for the café.

Good questions

Questions, Answered

What Lightning does today, and what it doesn’t yet.

Is Lightning ready to use?

Yes. It’s a beta, and it works with your real numbers today: import your statements, add your balances and use every screen. You may meet a rough edge; the release log lists what’s still open.

How do I start?

Download the Windows beta from Downloads, unzip it and open Lightning. Mohab’s sample year is there too, if you’d like to learn the steps before adding your own.

What will it cost?

Nothing to start, and nothing is ever locked. After seven days, Lightning asks now and then for a one-time payment. Pay when it’s worth it to you, and you own it forever.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows. The beta is a ZIP: unzip it and open Lightning. Other systems come later.

Is there a phone app?

Yes. The Android beta is available now as an APK; Google Play comes later. Your phone holds the ledger: add spending with one tap and let it read your bank SMS. Pair it once with your Windows PC to sit down with your numbers.

Does it read all my messages?

No. Only messages from your banks, and only after you allow it. Messages from people are never read, and each bank message waits in review: nothing posts until you say so.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. Lightning never asks for bank logins. Download a CSV from any bank or wallet, match its columns once, and review every row before it’s saved. How importing a statement works.

What goes online?

Your money data never does. It stays encrypted on your phone and your PC, which talk to each other over your own Wi-Fi. The only thing Lightning looks up online is the latest price of what you hold, such as listed shares by ticker symbol. Where your data lives.
