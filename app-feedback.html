What happened?

Add as many tickets as you need. Attach up to five screenshots to each ticket, either with the upload button or by pasting into its description. PNG screenshots are optimized without reducing image quality when possible.

  • Tell us where you were.
  • Choose bug or improvement.
  • Choose visual or technical.
  • Describe what you expected and what happened.

Leave these blank to stay anonymous. If you share an email, we will only use it to clarify this feedback. Screenshots are stored in a private Drive folder and are visible only to its authorized reviewers. Check them for account details before sending.
