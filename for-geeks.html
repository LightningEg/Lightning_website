02 / THE SOURCE

Good software should show its working.

Money software should be understandable, not a black box. We make Lightning’s code public so developers can inspect the calculations, question our decisions and help make it better.

The project is source-available, not OSI open source. Its PolyForm Noncommercial licence permits non-commercial use. Commercial use requires written permission.

03 / THE DIRECTION

What we want
to build.

A useful financial life is bigger than a list of transactions. We want Lightning to make three things easier to understand.

01

The whole picture

Bring accounts, savings, investments and everyday spending into one view.

02

What comes next

Connect today’s cash to tomorrow’s bills, commitments and plans.

03

Numbers you can trust

Keep calculations inspectable, money private, and decisions grounded in the records.

If that sounds worth building, the source is there to explore. Issues and focused contributions are welcome.
