01 / ROOT

What Is Lightning,
Underneath?

Not another spreadsheet disguised as a dashboard. A small, considered system for recording and understanding money.

README / WHY IT EXISTS● READABLE

One place for the money.
One source for the truth.

Lightning tracks the things that are often scattered: bank accounts, cash, wallets, gold, investments and a monthly plan.

The foundation is a real ledger. Plans don't change balances. Reports don't invent transactions. Numbers are derived from the records behind them.

Built in Egypt, with the way people here actually hold and use money in mind.

03 / PRINCIPLES

01 / PRECISION

Money is not

02 / INTEGRITY

Real money.
Real records.
