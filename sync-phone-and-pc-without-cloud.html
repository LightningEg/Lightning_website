How it works

Pair Once, Then Just Open It.

Three steps, and only the first needs any setting up.

  1. 1

    Pair Once

    Once

    Your phone shows a code; type it on your PC. Both screens then show the same six digits, such as 370 548, so you know it’s your phone.

  2. 2

    Borrow It

    Any time

    Open it on your PC with the same password as on your phone. It arrives with every edit you made on the go, and your phone reads while you work.

  3. 3

    Hand It Back

    On close

    Close Lightning near your phone and the ledger goes home. Away from it, your edits are sealed and wait for the next meeting.

Good questions

Questions, Answered

Does it sync over the internet?

No. Your phone and PC need to be on the same Wi-Fi. Nothing goes through our servers, because we don’t have any for your money data.

Can I edit on both at the same time?

No. One device writes at a time: while your PC borrows the ledger, your phone reads, and the ledger goes back to your phone when you close Lightning near it.

Which phone and PC do I need?

An Android phone and a Windows PC. The Android beta is an APK for now; Google Play comes later.

What if my phone is away when I close the PC?

Your edits are sealed on the PC and wait. The next time the two meet, the ledger goes home.
