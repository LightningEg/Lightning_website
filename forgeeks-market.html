                Use it in the app

                A theme you buy here also dresses Lightning on your PC and phone. Three steps, once per profile.

                1. Buy it here. It shows under Owned.
                2. On the theme’s card, press Get app code.
                3. In the app, open Settings › Appearance, paste the code and press Unlock. Do this once: your profile’s other devices unlock by themselves.

                Another profile needs its own unlock, at a quarter of the price. Unlocking needs the app update that adds Unlock a theme you bought.
