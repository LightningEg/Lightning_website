Good questions

Questions, Answered

What people ask before they start.

Is Lightning a free expense tracker for Egypt?

Yes. Every money feature in Lightning is free today: no expiring trial, subscription, licence fee or paywall. If you want to support development, you can buy an optional theme in the Marketplace. Themes change appearance only and never unlock any feature. See how the free expense tracker works.

Is Lightning ready to use?

Yes. Import your statements, add your balances and use every screen with your real numbers. We improve it with every release; the release log shows what changed.

How do I start?

Download Lightning for Windows or Android from Downloads. On Windows, unzip it and open Lightning; on Android, open the file and allow your browser to install it once. The sample year is there too, to learn the steps before adding your own.

Does it read all my messages?

No. Only messages from your banks, and only after you allow it. Messages from people are never read, and each bank message waits in review: nothing posts until you say so.

Does it connect to my bank, or send my data anywhere?

No. Lightning never asks for bank logins: download a CSV from any bank or wallet, match its columns once, and review every row. Your money data stays encrypted on your phone and PC. The only things looked up online are the latest prices and exchange rates for what you hold. How importing works · Where your data lives.

Can I track dollars and US shares?

Yes. Each account keeps its own currency, and Lightning counts it in EGP at the day’s rate, or the rate your bank gave you. The largest US stocks and ETFs on Nasdaq and NYSE get their prices by themselves. Gulf and European shares work too, with prices you type. See how dollar savings are tracked.
