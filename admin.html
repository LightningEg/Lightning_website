Owner tools

Overview

What needs you, how the month is going, and whether the store is ready to sell.

Verify your session to load the dashboard.

Top-ups

Oldest first. Find each one in your InstaPay or bank app, then confirm what arrived. The server credits only when it matches the quote.

Sales

What sold, how it was bought, and the carts that were filled but never checked out. Coins, not EGP: the Overview counts revenue from top-ups.

Verify your session to load sales.

Activity

The audit trail: every coin that moved, every score change, every sale and everything you did by hand, newest first. It reads the permanent records, so it cannot disagree with them.

Verify your session to load activity.

Catalogue

Every look on sale. A theme takes its tier’s price everywhere it is worn: both sites, the PC and the phone. Sets follow their parts at 80%.

Verify your session to load the catalogue.

Free a profile

Each purchase of an app theme unlocks it for one Lightning profile. Look a member up by email to see which profiles hold a theme, and free a seat for someone who lost a device or profile.

Members

Credit coins or recognition points by hand: a goodwill gift, a refund in coins, a contest prize. Paid top-ups are confirmed under Top-ups, never here.

Credit coins or recognition points

Coins fund purchases. Recognition points affect ranking but cannot be spent. This is a manual credit, not confirmation of an InstaPay payment. Give a clear reason.

Remove a profile picture

For a picture that breaks the community rules. It disappears from every page at once, your reason is kept in the audit log, and the member can upload another.

Community

Stories to review, merges held for people who have not linked GitHub yet, and what each reward pays. Open the post, check the member’s code and the link back to Lightning, then reward it once. Up to three rewarded posts per member in 30 days.

Held for linking

Merged pull requests and coins waiting for a GitHub account that is not linked to a Lightning account yet. Nothing is lost: when the author links GitHub in their account, what waits here is paid to them once.

Verify your session to load what is held.

Story rewards

What one press of Approve pays for a story, and the extra coins when the blog or a guide links to a published story. Coins are spendable; points raise the leaderboard.

Money journal

Every transfer recorded, with or without a ticket. Rarely needed: money that arrived with no top-up to match, or an entry to correct.

Record money that arrived with no ticket

Settings

How members pay, what a coin costs, and who runs the store.

Checkout

The QR is private and appears only in a signed-in member’s accepted checkout. Support: hello@lightningeg.com.

Upload only when changing the QR.

Coin price

Members type an EGP amount; the quote freezes the rate and bonus before they pay, so a change here only affects new top-ups.

Monthly fixed cost

The break-even line on the Overview: hosting, tools and your own time, in EGP a month.

Owner account

Only one account opens this console and sees its link in the menu.

Receipt retention

Delete eligible receipt images older than 90 days. Pending payment evidence is protected; ledger records stay.
