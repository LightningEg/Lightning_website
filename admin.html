Upload only when changing the QR.

Amount-based coin pricing

Customers enter an EGP amount. The quote freezes the rate and bonus before payment; later pricing changes affect new requests.

Record money received

Open your own InstaPay or bank account first. A customer receipt alone is not proof that the money arrived. Each receiving reference is recorded once.

Bank journal

Reconcile customer requests

Match the member email, sender address, receipt and receiving references. Multiple partial transfers can fund one request. Coins are credited only when the verified bank total equals the accepted quote.

Review social posts

Open the LinkedIn or Reddit post, check the member’s verification code and link back to Lightning, then choose coins and/or leaderboard points. A post can receive each reward once; a member can earn coins for at most three posts in 30 days.

Receipt retention

Delete eligible image files older than 90 days. Pending payment evidence is protected; financial ledger records remain.

Credit coins or recognition points

Coins fund purchases. Recognition points affect ranking but cannot be spent. This is a manual credit, not confirmation of an InstaPay payment. Give a clear reason.

Palette prices

Mono stays free. New locked starter palettes begin at 100 coins; change prices here without editing SQL.
