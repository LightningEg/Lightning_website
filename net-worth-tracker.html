What counts

Three Rules Keep the Number Honest.

Count everything once, at today’s value, and only what is yours.

Count Every Place

Bank accounts, Vodafone Cash, cash at home, certificates, THNDR shares and gold, each in EGP at its latest value.

Leave Out What Isn’t Yours

Money you keep for family sits in your account but isn’t yours. Mark who it’s held for and it stays out.

Take Off What You Owe

Loans, installments and bills already due. Mohab’s two loans, an office gam’eya and October’s rent, now late, come to 78,500 EGP.

Good questions

Net Worth, Answered

What people in Egypt ask when they first add it all up.

Should gold count in my net worth?

Yes. Gold is something you own and could sell. Lightning values each piece by its net weight and the price per gram for its karat, so an 18K ring and a 21K gold pound are each priced right.

Do bank certificates count?

Yes, at their balance, even before you can withdraw them. Mohab’s NBE three-year certificate counts as 100,000 EGP.

Why did my net worth go up when my bank balance went down?

Moving money from your bank into a certificate, gold or shares changes where it sits, not what you own. Lightning counts the move once, so net worth only changes with what you saved, what you spent and how your holdings did.

How often should I check it?

Once a month is enough. Import your statements at the end of the month and the Overview shows your net worth and how it changed. Mohab’s rose 311,804 EGP from January to early October. Your phone and your PC show the same figure: the phone holds one ledger and the PC borrows it over your own Wi-Fi.

How are shares valued?

For listed shares, Lightning looks up the latest price by ticker symbol. That is the only thing it looks up online. For gold and other holdings, you record the price.
