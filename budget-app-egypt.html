Good questions

Budgeting, Answered

Plain answers for a salary paid in EGP.

How much of my salary should I budget?

There is no one right share. Start from your real numbers: import two or three months of statements, see what you usually spend in each category, and plan from that. Then trim one category at a time.

Do bills count in the budget?

Yes. A scheduled bill in a category with a plan counts inside that plan, so rent is never counted twice.

What happens to money I don’t spend?

Turn on carryover for a category and what you didn’t use is added to next month’s plan. It moves no money; it only raises next month’s limit.

Does a budget move money?

No. A budget is a plan. Only what you record in your accounts moves money, and the budget compares the two.
